PAYE (Income Tax) Progressive income tax deducted by your employer. Rates: 10% (up to KES 24k), 25%, 30%, 32.5%, 35%. Personal relief of KES 2,400/month is subtracted. KRA source ↗
NSSF Contribution 6% of gross salary in two tiers: Tier I on first KES 8,000 and Tier II on next KES 64,000. Employer matches your contribution. NSSF source ↗
SHIF Contribution Social Health Insurance Fund (replaced NHIF in 2024). 2.75% of gross salary with no upper cap. Employer also contributes 2.75%. SHIF source ↗
Housing Levy (1.5%) Affordable Housing Levy introduced under Finance Act 2023. Employee pays 1.5% of gross salary; employer matches 1.5%. Funds affordable housing construction in Kenya.
Personal Relief A fixed tax credit of KES 2,400 per month (KES 28,800 per year) deducted from your PAYE before remittance. Every Kenyan employee earning taxable income is entitled to this relief.

Gross Salary vs Net Salary in Kenya

Gross salary is your total pay before deductions.
Net salary is what you actually receive after PAYE and statutory deductions.

Kenya Salary Calculator bridges this gap instantly, clearly showing every deduction line-by-line.
