KES 300,000 Salary After Tax — FAQs

What is the net pay for a KES 300,000 salary in Kenya?

A KES 300,000 gross monthly salary results in a net take-home pay of approximately KES 205,668 per month (KES 2,468,016 per year) after PAYE of KES 76,614, NSSF of KES 6,480 (capped), SHIF of KES 8,250, and Housing Levy of KES 4,500 are deducted.

How much PAYE tax do I pay on KES 300,000?

On a KES 300,000 gross monthly salary, your PAYE (income tax) is KES 76,614 per month. This is calculated on your taxable income of KES 280,770 (after NSSF, SHIF, and Housing Levy deductions), using the 2025 KRA tax bands, then personal relief of KES 2,400 is subtracted.

What statutory deductions apply to a KES 300,000 salary?

The statutory deductions are: NSSF (KES 6,480, capped), SHIF 2.75% (KES 8,250), Housing Levy 1.5% (KES 4,500), and PAYE (KES 76,614). Total deductions: KES 95,844.

How is the NSSF contribution calculated for KES 300,000?

Under the NSSF Act 2013, contributions are 6% of gross salary, capped at KES 6,480 per month (once gross salary reaches KES 108,000). For a KES 300,000 salary, the NSSF contribution is KES 6,480 per month (capped).
