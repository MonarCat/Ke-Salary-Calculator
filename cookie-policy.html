1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and provide information to the website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

Kenya Salary Calculator uses cookies to enhance your experience, provide our services, and understand how you use our website. We use cookies for the following purposes:

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, access to secure areas, and maintaining your session when you log in.

Examples:

  • Authentication cookies (to keep you logged in)
  • Session management cookies
  • Security cookies

2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences and settings.

Examples:

  • Language preferences
  • Recent calculations
  • User interface preferences

2.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve our service.

Examples:

  • Google Analytics cookies (if implemented)
  • Page view tracking
  • Feature usage statistics

2.4 Advertising Cookies

We currently do not use advertising cookies. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and seek your consent.

5. How to Control Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can control cookies through:

5.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies from being set
  • Set preferences for specific websites

Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

5.2 Browser Add-ons

You can also use browser add-ons or extensions to manage cookies and tracking:

  • Privacy Badger
  • uBlock Origin
  • Cookie AutoDelete

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly:

  • You will not be able to stay logged in to your account
  • Your preferences will not be saved
  • Some features may not work correctly
  • You may need to re-enter information more frequently

7. Local Storage and Session Storage

In addition to cookies, we also use browser local storage and session storage to:

Like cookies, you can clear local storage and session storage through your browser settings.

8. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals websites that you do not want to be tracked. Currently, there is no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals. We do not currently respond to DNT signals, but we respect your privacy choices and provide you with options to control cookies.
