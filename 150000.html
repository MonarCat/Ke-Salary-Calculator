KES 150,000 Salary After Tax — FAQs

What is the net pay for a KES 150,000 salary in Kenya?

A KES 150,000 gross monthly salary results in a net take-home pay of approximately KES 105,130 per month (KES 1,261,560 per year) after PAYE of KES 33,527, NSSF of KES 6,480 (capped), SHIF of KES 4,125, and Housing Levy of KES 2,250 are deducted.

How much PAYE tax do I pay on KES 150,000?

On a KES 150,000 gross monthly salary, your PAYE (income tax) is KES 33,527 per month. This is calculated on your taxable income of KES 137,145 (after NSSF, SHIF, and Housing Levy deductions), using the 2025 KRA tax bands, then personal relief of KES 2,400 is subtracted.

What statutory deductions apply to a KES 150,000 salary?

The statutory deductions are: NSSF (KES 6,480, capped), SHIF 2.75% (KES 4,125), Housing Levy 1.5% (KES 2,250), and PAYE (KES 33,527). Total deductions: KES 46,382.

How is the NSSF contribution calculated for KES 150,000?

Under the NSSF Act 2013, contributions are 6% of gross salary, capped at KES 6,480 per month (once gross salary reaches KES 108,000). For a KES 150,000 salary, the NSSF contribution is KES 6,480 per month (capped).
