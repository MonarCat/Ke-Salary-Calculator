SHIF 2025: How the Social Health Insurance Fund Affects Your Salary

From October 2024, SHIF replaced NHIF at 2.75% of gross salary. A KES 100,000 earner now pays KES 2,750/month instead of the old KES 1,700 — an extra KES 1,050 taken home each month.

Kenya PAYE Bands 2025/2026: Complete Explanation of All Tax Brackets

PAYE is deducted from your gross salary each month. Key bands: 10% on first KES 24,000 of taxable income; 25% up to KES 32,333; 30% up to KES 500,000; plus personal relief of KES 2,400/month.

Affordable Housing Levy Kenya: Who Pays, How Much, and What For?

The AHL is 1.5% of gross salary, upheld by the Supreme Court in 2024. A KES 50,000 earner pays KES 750/month; employer also contributes another KES 750. Funds go to the national housing programme.

How to Read Your Kenyan Payslip: Every Line Item Explained

Your payslip lists Gross Pay, then deductions: NSSF (6%, max KES 4,320), SHIF (2.75%), AHL (1.5%), and PAYE calculated on taxable income less KES 2,400 personal relief. Net Pay is what reaches your bank.
