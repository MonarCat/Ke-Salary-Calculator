Public Sector vs Private Sector Nurses in Kenya

🏛️ Public Sector (Government / TSC)

  • Gross salary: KES 40,000–90,000
  • Employer: Ministry of Health, County Governments
  • Benefits: Pension (NSSF + Civil Service scheme), group life insurance
  • Job security: High — permanent & pensionable
  • Allowances: House allowance, commuter allowance, risk allowance
  • Promotion path: Nursing Officer III → II → I → Senior

🏥 Private Sector (Hospitals & Clinics)

  • Gross salary: KES 50,000–130,000+
  • Employers: Aga Khan, Nairobi Hospital, MP Shah, Karen Hospital
  • Benefits: Private medical cover, performance bonuses
  • Pay: Generally higher than public for experienced nurses
  • Hours: May involve longer shifts and on-call duties
  • Specialization premium: ICU, Theatre, Oncology nurses earn more

Nursing Job Market in Kenya

  • Demand: High. Kenya has a significant shortage of registered nurses, especially in rural counties, creating strong demand.
  • Top Employers: Ministry of Health, County Health Departments, Aga Khan University Hospital, Nairobi Hospital, MP Shah Hospital, Mater Hospital, AAR, Karen Hospital.
  • Top Cities/Regions: Nairobi, Mombasa, Nakuru, Kisumu, Eldoret.
  • International opportunities: UK NHS, UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Canada actively recruit Kenyan nurses — overseas salaries can be 5–10× local rates.
  • Effective deduction rate: At KES 75,000 gross, you lose 26.9% to statutory deductions.
  • Annual net pay: KES 658,332 per year.

Nurse Salary Kenya — FAQs

What is the average nurse salary in Kenya in 2025?

The average gross salary for a registered nurse in Kenya is approximately KES 60,000–90,000 per month in 2025. Using KES 75,000 as the midpoint, after deductions (PAYE KES 12,577, NSSF KES 4,500, SHIF KES 2,063, Housing Levy KES 1,125), the net take-home pay is KES 54,735 per month.

How much does a government nurse earn in Kenya?

Government nurses employed by the Ministry of Health or county governments typically earn between KES 40,000 and KES 90,000 gross per month, depending on job group and years of service. This usually includes allowances such as house allowance, commuter allowance, and risk allowance, which are part of the total package but may be taxed separately.

Do nurses in Kenya pay PAYE tax?

Yes. All employed nurses pay PAYE (Pay As You Earn) income tax in Kenya. At a gross salary of KES 75,000, the PAYE tax is KES 12,631 per month (KES 151,572 per year), after the personal relief of KES 2,400/month is applied. Nurses also contribute to NSSF, SHIF, and the Housing Levy.

Can a Kenyan nurse work abroad and earn more?

Yes. Kenyan nurses are in high demand in the UK (NHS), UAE, Saudi Arabia, Canada, and Australia. UK NHS salaries start at around £28,000–£35,000 per year (KES 4.6M–5.7M). The Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC) in the UK and equivalent bodies in other countries run structured recruitment programmes for internationally qualified nurses.
