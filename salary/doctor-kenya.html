Public Sector vs Private Sector Doctors in Kenya

🏛️ Public Sector (Ministry of Health / County)

  • Medical Intern: KES 70,000–100,000 (government stipend)
  • Medical Officer: KES 120,000–200,000
  • Senior Medical Officer: KES 200,000–300,000
  • Benefits: Civil service pension, group insurance
  • Job security: Permanent & pensionable
  • Allowances: On-call, risk, house, commuter allowances

🏥 Private Sector (Hospitals & Clinics)

  • Medical Officer: KES 200,000–350,000
  • Specialist: KES 300,000–600,000+
  • Consultant / Locum: KES 500,000–1,000,000+
  • Top employers: Aga Khan, Nairobi Hospital, MP Shah, Karen
  • Pay: Significantly higher than public sector
  • Private practice: Potential for much higher income

Doctor Job Market in Kenya

  • Demand: Very high, particularly in underserved counties. Kenya has approximately 1 doctor per 5,000 people, far below the WHO recommended 1:1,000.
  • Top Employers: Ministry of Health, County Health Departments, Aga Khan University Hospital, Nairobi Hospital, MP Shah Hospital, Mater Hospital, Kenyatta National Hospital, Karen Hospital, Médecins Sans Frontières, UN agencies.
  • Top Cities: Nairobi (highest salaries), Mombasa, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisumu.
  • Specializations in demand: Surgery, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Anaesthesiology, Radiology, Oncology, Paediatrics.
  • Effective deduction rate: At KES 250,000 gross, you lose 31.1% to statutory deductions — one of the highest rates due to the 30% PAYE bracket.
  • Annual net pay: KES 2,065,860 per year.

Doctor Salary Kenya — FAQs

What is the average doctor salary in Kenya in 2025?

A medical officer in Kenya earns approximately KES 150,000–300,000 gross per month in 2025. Using KES 250,000 as a mid-range figure, after deductions (PAYE KES 62,900, NSSF KES 4,320, SHIF KES 6,875, Housing Levy KES 3,750), the net take-home pay is KES 172,155 per month (KES 2,065,860 annually).

How much PAYE tax does a doctor pay in Kenya?

On a KES 250,000 gross salary, a doctor's taxable income after NSSF (KES 4,320), SHIF (KES 6,875), and Housing Levy (KES 3,750) is KES 235,055. Applying the 2025 KRA tax bands (25% on KES 32,333–57,333, 30% above KES 57,333) and deducting personal relief of KES 2,400 yields PAYE of KES 62,900 per month — the highest deduction by far.

Do private sector doctors earn significantly more than government doctors in Kenya?

Yes, significantly. Government medical officers typically earn KES 120,000–200,000 per month, while equivalent roles in top private hospitals like Aga Khan or Nairobi Hospital pay KES 200,000–350,000. Consultants and specialists in private practice can earn KES 500,000–1,000,000+ per month, particularly when combining employed income with locum and private clinic earnings.

What specializations earn the highest salaries for doctors in Kenya?

The highest-paying medical specializations in Kenya are: Cardiothoracic Surgery, Neurosurgery, Radiology (especially interventional), Anaesthesiology, Ophthalmology, and Orthopaedic Surgery. Specialists in these fields at senior levels in private practice can earn KES 500,000–2,000,000+ per month. These specializations typically require an additional 3–5 years of MMed or Fellowship training after the MBChB.
