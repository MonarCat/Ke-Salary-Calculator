Banker / Bank Officer Job Market in Kenya

  • Demand: High. Kenya's banking sector is one of Africa's most developed, with over 40 licensed commercial banks plus DFIs, SACCOs, and microfinance institutions actively recruiting.
  • Top Employers: Equity Bank, KCB Group, Co-operative Bank, Standard Chartered, Absa Kenya, NCBA, DTBK, Family Bank, SBIC Africa (Stanbic), I&M Bank, HFC, central Bank of Kenya.
  • Top Cities: Nairobi (dominant), Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, other commercial centres.
  • Key skills: Credit analysis, relationship management, AML/KYC, Treasury, trade finance, digital banking, CPA / ACCA (for finance roles), AKIB certification.
  • Effective deduction rate: At KES 100,000 gross, you lose 28.4% of your salary to statutory deductions.
  • Annual net pay: KES 859,416 per year.

Banker / Bank Officer Salary Kenya — FAQs

What is the average banker salary in Kenya in 2025?

The average gross salary for a mid-level bank officer in Kenya is approximately KES 80,000–150,000 per month in 2025. A branch-level officer earning KES 100,000 gross takes home KES 71,618 per month after statutory deductions.

How much PAYE does a banker pay in Kenya?

On a KES 100,000 gross salary, a bank employee pays KES 19,812 in PAYE per month. Taxable income is KES 91,430 after NSSF (KES 4,320), SHIF (KES 2,750), and Housing Levy (KES 1,500) deductions.

Do bankers in Kenya receive bonuses?

Yes. Most Kenyan banks offer annual performance bonuses, which can range from one month's salary to 20%+ of annual gross pay depending on bank performance and individual targets. Bonuses are subject to PAYE.

What is the salary for a bank teller in Kenya?

A bank teller (cash officer) in Kenya typically earns KES 40,000–70,000 gross per month. After statutory deductions, take-home pay is approximately KES 32,000–52,000 per month. Entry-level graduate trainees at large banks may start at KES 50,000–65,000.
