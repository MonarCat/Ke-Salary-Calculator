Civil / Structural Engineer Job Market in Kenya

  • Demand: Very High. Kenya's infrastructure push (roads, housing, LAPSSET corridor) and real estate development are driving strong demand for qualified engineers.
  • Top Employers: Kenya National Highways Authority (KeNHA), NCA-registered contractors, Kengen, KPLC, Safaricom, consultancies (Strabag, Knight Frank, Atkins), county governments.
  • Top Cities: Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret.
  • In-demand skills: Structural engineering, water and sanitation, roads & highways, project management, geotechnical engineering, BIM/AutoCAD skills.
  • Effective deduction rate: At KES 130,000 gross, you lose 29.4% of your salary to statutory deductions.
  • Annual net pay: KES 1,100,700 per year.

Civil / Structural Engineer Salary Kenya — FAQs

What is the average civil engineer salary in Kenya in 2025?

The average gross monthly salary for a civil engineer in Kenya is approximately KES 100,000–160,000 in 2025. After statutory deductions, a KES 130,000 gross salary yields a net take-home of KES 91,725. Senior engineers and project managers earn significantly more.

How much PAYE does a civil engineer pay on KES 130,000?

On a KES 130,000 gross salary, a civil engineer pays KES 28,430 in PAYE per month (KES 341,160 annually). Taxable income is KES 120,155 after NSSF (KES 4,320), SHIF (KES 3,575), and Housing Levy (KES 1,950), then personal relief of KES 2,400 is subtracted.

What qualifications do engineers need in Kenya?

Engineers in Kenya must hold at least a BSc in Civil Engineering from an accredited university. Registration with the Engineers Board of Kenya (EBK) is mandatory for practicing engineers. The Professional Engineer (PE) designation requires 3+ years of experience and passing an EBK exam.

Do engineers working for donors or NGOs earn more?

Yes, engineers working for international NGOs or on donor-funded projects (World Bank, AfDB, USAID) often earn 50–100% more than those in local employment. Additionally, consulting engineers billing hourly can earn the equivalent of KES 200,000–500,000/month at senior levels.
