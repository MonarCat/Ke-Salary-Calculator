Bank Officer Salary Kenya — FAQs

What is the average bank officer salary in Kenya in 2025?

The average gross monthly salary for a bank officer in Kenya ranges from KES 60,000–120,000 depending on level and bank tier. After statutory deductions, a KES 85,000 gross salary yields a net take-home of KES 61,564. Tier 1 banks typically pay 20–30% more than Tier 3 banks.

How much PAYE does a bank officer pay on KES 85,000?

On a KES 85,000 gross salary, a bank officer pays KES 15,503 in PAYE per month (KES 186,036 annually). Taxable income is KES 77,067 after NSSF (KES 4,320), SHIF (KES 2,338), and Housing Levy (KES 1,275), then personal relief of KES 2,400 is subtracted.

Do bank employees get additional benefits beyond salary?

Yes, Kenyan bank employees typically receive benefits including medical cover (often for family), staff loans at preferential rates, pension schemes beyond statutory NSSF, performance bonuses, and housing/transport allowances. These benefits can add 20–40% to the total compensation package.

What qualifications are needed for banking jobs in Kenya?

Most banks require at least a bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, economics, or business. Professional qualifications like ACCA, CPA(K), or CFA are highly valued. The Kenya Bankers Association also offers the Banking Certificate Program (BCP) for entry-level banking professionals.
