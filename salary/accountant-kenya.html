Accounting Job Market in Kenya

  • Demand: Consistently high. Every registered company requires accounting services; demand is strong in banking, NGOs, government, and manufacturing.
  • Top Employers: Deloitte, PwC, KPMG, EY (Big 4), Equity Bank, KCB, Safaricom, Kenya Power, UN agencies, international NGOs (World Bank, USAID contractors).
  • Top Cities: Nairobi (dominant), Mombasa, Nakuru, Kisumu.
  • Key Certifications: CPA-K (ICPAK), ACCA, CFA (for finance/investment roles). CPA-K is the most valued local qualification.
  • Effective deduction rate: At KES 80,000 gross, you lose 27.2% to statutory deductions.
  • Annual net pay: KES 698,556 per year.

Accountant Salary Kenya — FAQs

What is the average accountant salary in Kenya in 2025?

The average gross salary for an accountant in Kenya is approximately KES 70,000–90,000 per month in 2025 (around KES 80,000 mid-range). After statutory deductions of KES 21,787 (PAYE KES 14,067, NSSF KES 4,320, SHIF KES 2,200, Housing Levy KES 1,200), the net take-home pay is KES 58,213 per month (KES 698,556 annually).

Does CPA-K certification significantly increase accountant salaries in Kenya?

Yes. A fully qualified CPA-K (Certified Public Accountant of Kenya) can earn 30–60% more than an unqualified accountant at the same experience level. ICPAK membership also opens doors to senior roles, NGO positions, and Big 4 firms. The CPA-K qualification is administered by KASNEB and is the most widely recognised accounting credential in Kenya.

How much does a Big 4 accountant earn in Kenya?

Associates at Deloitte, PwC, KPMG, and EY Kenya typically start at KES 80,000–130,000 gross per month. Senior Associates earn KES 130,000–200,000, and Managers can earn KES 200,000–350,000. Partner-level compensation is significantly higher and often includes profit-sharing.

What are the PAYE deductions for a KES 80,000 accountant salary?

On a KES 80,000 gross salary, the taxable income after NSSF (KES 4,320), SHIF (KES 2,200), and Housing Levy (KES 1,200) deductions is KES 72,280. Applying the 2025 KRA tax bands and subtracting personal relief of KES 2,400 yields PAYE of KES 14,067 per month (KES 168,804 per year).
