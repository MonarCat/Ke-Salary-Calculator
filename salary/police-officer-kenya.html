Police Officer (Kenya Police Service) Job Market in Kenya

  • Demand: Stable. Kenya Police Service regularly recruits constables and specialist officers. Promotions are rank-based within the National Police Service.
  • Top Employers: Kenya Police Service, Administration Police Service, Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI), Kenya Prisons Service, National Intelligence Service (NIS).
  • Top Cities: Nationwide deployment. Headquarters in Nairobi.
  • Key skills: Law enforcement, crime investigation, community policing, firearms proficiency, Kenya National Police Service Act compliance.
  • Effective deduction rate: At KES 55,000 gross, you lose 23.3% of your salary to statutory deductions.
  • Annual net pay: KES 506,040 per year.

Police Officer (Kenya Police Service) Salary Kenya — FAQs

What is the average police officer salary in Kenya in 2025?

The average gross salary for a police officer (sergeant / inspector grade) in Kenya is approximately KES 50,000–80,000 per month in 2025. A mid-grade officer earning KES 55,000 gross takes home KES 42,170 per month after statutory deductions.

How much PAYE does a Kenyan police officer pay?

On a KES 55,000 gross salary, a police officer pays KES 7,192 in PAYE per month. Taxable income is KES 49,362 after NSSF (KES 3,300), SHIF (KES 1,512), and Housing Levy (KES 825) deductions.

Do Kenya police officers get housing allowance?

Yes. Kenya Police officers receive a housing allowance or are provided with government quarters. The housing allowance varies by rank and duty station. Allowances are part of gross pay and subject to statutory deductions.

What is the starting salary for a police constable in Kenya?

A new police constable in Kenya earns approximately KES 26,000–35,000 gross per month. After statutory deductions, take-home pay is approximately KES 22,000–29,000 per month, depending on allowances and duty station.
