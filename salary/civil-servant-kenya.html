Civil Servant / Government Employee Salary Kenya — FAQs

What is the average Kenya government salary in 2025?

Kenya civil servants earn according to the SRC (Salaries and Remuneration Commission) pay scales. Entry-level officers (Grade B–D) earn KES 30,000–55,000 gross. Mid-level officers (Grade E–G) earn KES 55,000–120,000. After statutory deductions on KES 70,000 gross, the net take-home is KES 51,594.

Do government employees pay PAYE in Kenya?

Yes, all Kenya government employees pay PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, and Housing Levy. On a KES 70,000 gross salary, PAYE is KES 11,231 per month. Government employees also contribute to the Public Service Superannuation Scheme (PSSS) for pension.

What are the benefits of working for the Kenya government?

Kenya government employees typically receive medical insurance through Jubilee or other approved schemes (NHIF historically), pension under PSSS, housing allowance, commuter allowance, and job security. The pension benefit is particularly valued, offering defined benefits upon retirement.

How does the SRC set civil servant salaries?

The Salaries and Remuneration Commission (SRC) reviews government pay scales periodically. The Unified Pay Scale (UNES) was introduced to harmonise pay across the public service. Salary grades are set based on job evaluation, experience, and comparison with private sector benchmarks.
