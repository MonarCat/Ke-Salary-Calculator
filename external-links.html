External Links

Official websites of key Kenyan government agencies relevant to payroll, tax compliance and social security.

Disclaimer: These links lead to external, independent government websites. Kenya Salary Calculator is not affiliated with these agencies. Always verify information directly on the official site.

Tax & Revenue

Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)

Official tax authority for Kenya. File returns, pay taxes, check PAYE obligations and get iTax PIN.

Visit KRA

iTax Portal (KRA)

Online portal for filing PAYE returns, VAT, income tax and downloading tax compliance certificates.

Open iTax

Social Security & Health

NSSF – National Social Security Fund

Manages pension savings for Kenyan employees. Check contributions, Tier I & II obligations and benefits.

Visit NSSF

SHIF – Social Health Insurance Fund

Replaced NHIF in 2024. Manages health insurance contributions (2.75% of gross salary).

Visit SHIF

NHIF – National Hospital Insurance Fund

Previous health insurer, now transitioning to SHIF. Access historical contribution records.

Visit NHIF

Housing & Infrastructure

Kenya Affordable Housing Programme

Government initiative for affordable housing funded by the Housing Levy (1.5% of gross salary).

Visit AHP

National Housing Corporation

Develops and manages housing in Kenya. Relevant to housing levy administration and beneficiaries.

Visit NHC

Labour & Employment

Ministry of Labour & Social Protection

Sets employment standards, minimum wage, labour laws and occupational safety regulations in Kenya.

Visit Ministry

Employment & Labour Relations Court

Resolves employment disputes, enforces labour laws and handles matters related to the Employment Act.

Visit ELRC

Federation of Kenya Employers (FKE)

Represents employers in Kenya. Provides advisory services on employment policy, HR and labour relations.

Visit FKE

Finance & Banking

Central Bank of Kenya (CBK)

Regulates banks, sets monetary policy and maintains financial stability in Kenya.

Visit CBK

National Treasury Kenya

Manages public finances, publishes Finance Acts, and oversees Kenya's budget and fiscal policies.

Visit Treasury

Capital Markets Authority (CMA)

Regulates Kenya's capital markets, including securities exchanges and investment funds.

Visit CMA

Government Services

eCitizen Kenya

Official government services portal for IDs, passports, business registration, driving licences and more.

Visit eCitizen

Huduma Kenya

One-stop government service centres providing access to multiple government services under one roof.

Visit Huduma

Business Registration Service (BRS)

Register businesses, companies and NGOs in Kenya. Check company status and certificates.

Visit BRS

Kenya Law

Official source of Kenyan legislation, case law, and legal information. Access Employment Act and Finance Acts.

Visit Kenya Law

Regulatory Bodies

Salaries & Remuneration Commission (SRC)

Sets pay structures for public sector employees and advises on remuneration policy in Kenya.

Visit SRC

Retirement Benefits Authority (RBA)

Regulates pension schemes and retirement funds in Kenya to protect members' interests.

Visit RBA

Office of the Data Protection Commissioner

Enforces Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Relevant for HR systems handling personal employee data.

Visit ODPC
