What Must Be Included on a Kenyan Payslip?

According to Kenyan employment law and best practices, a compliant payslip should include:

Employer Information:

  • Company/Employer name
  • Company address
  • Company KRA PIN

Employee Information:

  • Employee full name
  • Employee ID/number
  • Employee KRA PIN
  • NSSF number
  • Job title/designation
  • Department

Pay Period Information:

  • Month and year of payment
  • Pay date
  • Number of days worked (if applicable)

Earnings Breakdown:

  • Basic salary
  • House allowance
  • Transport allowance
  • Other allowances/benefits
  • Gross salary (total of all earnings)

Statutory Deductions:

  • PAYE (Pay As You Earn tax)
  • NSSF (pension contribution)
  • SHIF (health insurance)
  • Housing Levy

Other Deductions:

  • Pension contributions (if any)
  • SACCO contributions
  • Loan repayments
  • Advances
  • Other authorized deductions

Final Amount:

  • Net Salary (gross minus all deductions)
  • Payment method
  • Bank account details (partial, for verification)

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Calculations Don't Match My Expected Amount

Solution: Ensure you've entered the correct gross salary and all allowances. Remember that PAYE uses progressive tax bands, not a flat rate. Use our PAYE calculator to understand the breakdown.

Logo Doesn't Upload

Solution: Ensure your logo image is in JPG, PNG, or GIF format and less than 2MB in size. For best results, use a square or rectangular logo with transparent background.

Payslip Doesn't Print Properly

Solution: Download the PDF version instead of printing directly from the browser. PDFs maintain formatting across all devices and printers.

Can't Find My Downloaded Payslip

Solution: Check your browser's download folder. The file will be named "payslip-[employee-name]-[month-year].pdf"

Deductions Seem Too High

Solution: Verify that your gross salary is entered correctly. Remember that all allowances are subject to statutory deductions. Visit our Statutory Deductions page to understand each deduction.

Best Practices for Employers

  • Consistency: Use the same format for all employees' payslips
  • Timeliness: Generate and distribute payslips on or before payday
  • Record Keeping: Keep copies of all payslips for at least 5 years (tax requirement)
  • Transparency: Clearly show all earnings and deductions
  • Accuracy: Double-check KRA PINs, NSSF numbers, and bank details
  • Security: Ensure payslips are delivered securely (sealed envelope or encrypted email)
  • Compliance: Ensure all statutory remittances match the deductions shown
  • Employee Access: Provide employees with easy access to current and historical payslips

Employer Responsibility: While this tool generates compliant payslips, employers are responsible for ensuring all statutory deductions are correctly calculated and remitted to the appropriate authorities (KRA, NSSF, SHIF). Failure to remit deductions is a criminal offense under Kenyan law.
