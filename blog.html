Kenya Treasury Hints at Tax Abolition for Low-Income Earners Below KES 30,000

Admin 17 February 2025 PAYE • Tax Relief • KRA

Kenya’s National Treasury, under Cabinet Secretary John Mbadi, has hinted at abolishing PAYE income tax for workers earning below KES 30,000 per month. This landmark proposal could add over KES 1,000 to the monthly take-home pay of millions of low-income Kenyans.
