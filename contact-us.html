Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the salary calculations?
Our calculations are based on current Kenyan tax laws and KRA regulations. We regularly update our system to reflect any changes. However, we recommend verifying calculations with a qualified accountant for official purposes.
Is the service really free?
Yes! All our core features including salary calculations, payslip generation, and employee management are completely free. There are no hidden charges or premium tiers.
How do I generate payslips for multiple employees?
Create a free account, add your employees to the system, and use our bulk payslip generation feature. You can generate and email payslips to all employees with just a few clicks.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption to protect your data. All information is securely stored and we never share your personal or business data with third parties. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.
Can I customize the payslips with my company logo?
Yes! You can customize payslips with your company name, logo, and branding. This feature is available to all registered users at no extra cost.
What if I find an error in the calculations?
Please contact us immediately if you notice any discrepancies. We take accuracy very seriously and will investigate and correct any issues promptly. Your feedback helps us improve the service for everyone.
Do you offer training or support for employers?
Yes! We provide online support to help you get started. Use our contact form above. We also have resources and guides in our About Us page to help you make the most of our platform.
