Frequently Asked Questions

How does billing work?
Subscriptions are billed monthly. Your card will be charged automatically each month unless you cancel. You can cancel anytime, and you'll retain access until the end of your billing period.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes! You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time, and the difference will be prorated. If you downgrade, the change will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, and bank transfers through our secure payment partner, Paystack. All transactions are encrypted and secure.
Is there a free trial for premium plans?
Currently, we offer a free tier with 2 payslip downloads per month. This allows you to test the service before committing to a paid plan. Contact us if you'd like to discuss a trial period for Premium or Enterprise.
Can I get a refund?
We offer refunds within 7 days of your first payment if you're not satisfied with the service. After that, cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period with no refund for the remaining days.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data remains secure and accessible until the end of your billing period. After cancellation, you'll be downgraded to the free tier, but your employee data and calculation history will be preserved for 90 days.
Do you offer discounts for annual subscriptions?
Yes! Contact us at support@salarycalculator.co.ke to inquire about annual subscription discounts (typically 15-20% off).
