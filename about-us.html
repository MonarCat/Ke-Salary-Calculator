About Kenya Salary Calculator

Empowering Kenyans with transparent and accurate salary information

Our Story

Kenya Salary Calculator was born from a simple observation: many Kenyan employees and employers struggled to understand salary calculations, statutory deductions, and compliance with KRA regulations. What should be straightforward often became confusing, leading to errors, disputes, and uncertainty.

We created this platform to demystify salary calculations and make payroll management accessible to everyone. Whether you're an employee wanting to understand your payslip, a small business owner managing payroll, or an accountant serving multiple clients, our tools are designed to make your life easier.

Our Mission

To provide free, accurate, and easy-to-use salary calculation tools that empower Kenyan workers and businesses to understand and manage payroll with confidence.

Our Vision

To become Kenya's most trusted resource for salary calculations and payroll management, fostering transparency and trust in employment relationships.

What We Offer

Our comprehensive suite of tools is designed to meet all your payroll needs:

  • Free Salary Calculator: Instantly calculate net salary with all statutory deductions
  • PAYE Calculator: Understand your tax obligations based on current KRA rates
  • Statutory Deductions Calculator: Calculate NSSF, SHIF, and Housing Levy accurately
  • Payslip Generator: Create professional, customizable payslips in minutes
  • Employee Management: Manage multiple employees and their payroll records
  • Bulk Processing: Generate payslips for multiple employees at once
  • Email Distribution: Send payslips directly to employees
